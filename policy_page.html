Privacy Policy

App: HD Video Player All Format (Android, package hd.video.player.all.format.videoplayer)
Developer: VK Info
Effective date: November 22, 2026

In short: HD Video Player All Format plays videos that are already stored on your device. Your videos never leave your device. We (VK Info) do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data ourselves, and the app has no user accounts.

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect some device information, as described in section 6.

1. Information we collect

We (VK Info) do not collect any personal information, and we do not run servers that receive data from the app.

The only data that leaves your device is the information Google AdMob collects to show and measure ads (see section 6). Your videos, file names and subtitle files are never sent anywhere.

2. Information the app uses on your device

To work as a video player, the app reads the following on your device. This information is processed only on your device and never leaves it:

  • Your video files and their basic details (file name, folder, size, duration), so the app can list your videos by folder, show thumbnails, and play them.
  • Subtitle files (.srt) that you choose yourself, to show subtitles while a video plays.

3. Information stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of information in its own private storage on your device:

  • Thumbnail images of your videos, so lists load faster.
  • App settings, such as your chosen sort order and the last folder you opened.

This information stays on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Actions you choose

  • Rename and Delete change or remove a video file on your device, only after you confirm.
  • Share opens Android's share menu. The video is sent only to the app you pick, and that app's own privacy policy applies to it.
  • Brightness controls in the player change the brightness of the player screen only. They do not change your device's system settings.

5. Permissions

PermissionWhy it is used
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO To find, list and play the videos stored on your device. This is the app's core function. You can grant or revoke it at any time in Android Settings.
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used only by Google AdMob to load and display ads. Your videos and files are never uploaded.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID Lets Google AdMob read the Android advertising ID to show and measure ads (see section 6).
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Declared by a file-picker library included in the app, which is used to choose subtitle files. These permissions have no effect on Android 13 and newer, which the app requires.
WAKE_LOCK Used by the video playback library to keep playback running smoothly.

6. Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, an advertising service from Google. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • The Android advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Device and app information (for example device model, operating system version, app version)
  • IP address, and approximate location derived from it
  • How you interact with ads (for example ad views and clicks)
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data related to ads

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and Google's use of it is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Ads may be personalized based on this information unless you opt out. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you will be asked for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

Your choices: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads).

7. Third-party services and libraries

  • Google AdMob: shows ads, as described in section 6.
  • ExoPlayer / AndroidX Media3 (by Google) and Glide: open-source libraries that play videos and show thumbnails. They work only with files on your device and do not send any data to us or to third parties.

The app does not contain any analytics services.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us using the details below.

9. Data security and deletion

We do not hold any of your data, so there is nothing stored with us to delete. Information stored by the app on your device (see section 3) can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. For information collected by Google AdMob, you can reset or delete your advertising ID as described in section 6, and manage your data with Google at myaccount.google.com.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app changes. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. If a future version of the app starts collecting or sharing any additional data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
